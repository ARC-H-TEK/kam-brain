# Code of Conduct

## Operator covenant

This repo is maintained by a single operator. The covenant below governs how I expect to be approached, and how I will respond.

### What I value

- **Directness.** Tell me what you want, why, and what you would do if I said no.
- **Specifics.** "Could you help me with X" is better than "we should collaborate."
- **Time-zone awareness.** I am in Europe/Berlin. Bauleister hours (06:30–17:00) are off-limits for trade conversations.
- **Charter alignment.** If your offer does not map to one of the revenue channels in [CHARTER.md §5](CHARTER.md), it is not a priority.

### What I will not do

- **Work on martingale, grid, recovery, or average-down strategies.** Not negotiable. Even a "small one for testing."
- **Install cracked EAs or follow cracked Telegram groups.** The msimg32.dll pattern is a red flag I will shut down immediately.
- **Approve any spending that is not mapped to a revenue channel.** [Charter §2 ⚠️](CHARTER.md) is the gate.
- **Promise delivery dates.** I build when the architecture is clear. Otherwise I park.

### How to start a conversation

Email `tekdag2944@gmail.com` with:

1. Subject line describing the request.
2. What you want.
3. Why you want it.
4. What you would do if I said no.

### If I say no

I will say no. I will not negotiate. Move on.

### If you disagree with the charter

Open an issue or send an email. I will read it. I will probably not change the charter. Hard constraints are not subject to feedback.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All commits, issues, pull requests, and comments on this repo.
- All email correspondence initiated from this repo.
- All Telegram / DM contact from accounts linked in the charter.

It does not apply to private infrastructure, which is not public.

## Enforcement

The operator is the only enforcement authority. Mistakes on my side will be acknowledged publicly if the upstream is verifiable.
